LATEST NEWS · NOVEMBER 2026

Evaluation of the UN Cooperation Framework 2021–2025

SRA, as the national consulting firm, and Act for Performance, as the international firm, were the implementing partners for the evaluation of the Cooperation Framework between the Government of Somalia and the United Nations. SRA moderated the validation workshop with government and UN stakeholders, and the report has been published.
